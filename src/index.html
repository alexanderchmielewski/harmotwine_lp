Good to know

A few quiet answers.

How much can I play for free?

The first two sound worlds, with 24 puzzles in total, are free to play without video ads. Unlock later levels through optional rewarded videos or the one-time Pro purchase. Pro is not a subscription. Gameplay progression requirements still apply.

Will ads interrupt my game?

No video ads appear automatically. From the third world onward, you choose whether to watch an available video to unlock a pack of up to five levels. With Pro, you do not need videos to unlock those levels.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can play accessible puzzles offline. Videos, purchases and restoring Pro require an internet connection and an available store.

Can I continue on a new device?

Progress, your collection and video unlocks are saved locally. There is no dedicated cloud sync. You can restore Pro using the same store account through the app's restore purchases option. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google.
